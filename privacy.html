ModiDex privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

ModiDex is a free, fan-made daily game. It has no ads, and we never sell or share your information. Here's exactly what it stores.

If you just play

  • Your progress (Dex, stats, today's and past games, badges, partner and gym progress) is saved only in your browser on this device, using local storage. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Like most websites, the page loads fonts from Google Fonts and code from Google's servers, and is hosted on GitHub Pages. Those services can see your IP address when your browser loads files from them.
  • There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It uses Google's Firebase service, either with your Google account or a link sent to your email.

  • Your account: Firebase stores your email address and a random account ID. If you sign in with Google, it also receives your Google display name. A sign-in token is kept in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Your saved progress: the same progress described above is copied to Firebase, so it syncs across your devices. Only you can read it.
  • Your public profile: if you choose a username, your friends and the members of groups you join can see your username, Dex, badges, partner, streaks and puzzle scores (never the answers). Your email address is never shown to anyone.
  • Friends, groups and trades: friend requests and trade offers store both players' usernames until they're answered. Groups store their name, join code and members.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in: open your profile (the 👤 button), then Me → Delete my profile. This deletes your saved progress, username, public profile and group memberships from Firebase, and your account.
  • This device: clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove progress saved on the device.

Children

ModiDex is fine for all ages to play, but signing in is meant for players 13 and older. If you're under 13, please play without signing in.
